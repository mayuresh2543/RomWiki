    Troubleshooting & FAQ

    If you run into any issues during installation or daily usage, consult the solutions below.

    ⚠️ Common Flashing Errors

    Device Bootloops or boots straight back into Recovery

    Why it happens: - You did not format data during a clean flash (encrypting / mismatching existing data). - You flashed a separate GApps zip onto a build that already has GApps included. - You flashed an incompatible custom kernel or Magisk module.

    Solution: 1. Force power off by holding ++power++ + ++volume-up++ until entering recovery mode. 2. Go to Factory Reset ➔ Format data / factory reset and confirm. 3. Reboot to system.

    Error 1 / Verification Failed during adb sideload

    Why it happens: - The downloaded ROM file was corrupted during download. - An outdated or incompatible recovery is being used.

    Solution: 1. Verify the SHA256 checksum or re-download the ROM zip file. 2. Flash the recommended recovery image explicitly specified in the device's install guide.

    Device is not detected by fastboot or adb

    Why it happens: - Missing or corrupt Google USB / Xiaomi Fastboot drivers on Windows. - Faulty USB cable or USB 3.0/3.1 port handshake issue.

    Solution: 1. Download and install the Google USB Driver. 2. Use a USB 2.0 port or a USB 2.0 hub (USB 3.x ports on AMD motherboards often drop fastboot connections). 3. Verify detection in terminal: 

    fastboot devices

    📋 How to Capture a Logcat for Bug Reports

    When reporting crashes or issues in the Telegram group, providing a log is essential for debugging:

    1. Enable Developer Options (tap Build Number 7 times in Settings ➔ About Phone).
    2. Enable USB Debugging.
    3. Connect your phone to your computer and run: 
      adb logcat -d > logcat.txt
    4. Send the generated logcat.txt in the group with a clear description of the bug.
