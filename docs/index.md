## Requirements

Before flashing any build, ensure you meet the following prerequisites:

- **Platform Tools** — Install the latest [ADB & Fastboot tools](https://developer.android.com/studio/releases/platform-tools).
- **Bootloader** — Must be unlocked for your device.
- **Firmware** — Must be on the latest official firmware for your device and region.
- **Backups** — Formatting data is required; backup all critical data externally.
- **Recovery** — Use only the recommended recovery specified in your device's installation guide.

!!! warning "Custom Kernels Not Supported"
    Do not flash custom kernels. Any bugs reported on custom kernels will not be addressed.
